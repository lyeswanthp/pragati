Art. no. 001 · First date

Dear Pragati,

Will you go on an IKEA date with me?

We have been friends for so long. You already know how I treat you, how I respect you, and the way I see you. We have watched movies together and eaten out together, and every one of those days felt easy.

But something has changed. We are not just friends anymore. We are seeing each other, and I want to begin this chapter the right way.

So for our first date, I want to show you who I am. My taste. How long I can happily wander beside you while you shop. How well I listen to you, and how gently I try to convince you. I can think of no better place for that than IKEA, with its hundred little rooms to get lost in.

Let it be a day where you get to know me a little more, so that the two of us can keep moving forward, together.

Pssst, one of these buttons is a little shy.
